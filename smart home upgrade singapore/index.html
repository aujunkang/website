Discuss Your Smart Home Upgrade

What Would You Like to Improve?

Start with the parts of daily life that are not working as they should. A clear list of problems helps shape an upgrade around useful outcomes, rather than adding devices without a practical reason.

  • Outdated controls: Existing controls may feel difficult to use or no longer suit the household’s routines.
  • Unreliable automation: Lighting scenes or other controls may behave inconsistently, or the system may be difficult to operate.
  • WiFi and network issues: Weak coverage, dead zones or unstable connections can affect work, streaming and connected systems.
  • Entertainment improvements: You may want to update AV equipment, improve room control or rethink a home theatre experience.
  • Security needs: CCTV surveillance, intercom or other security requirements may have changed since the original installation.
  • Lighting and curtains: You may want lighting scenes, dimming or motorised curtains to work more naturally with your home. HAP also offers Lutron Lighting.
  • System expansion: You may be considering additional technologies within an existing integrated system.

HAP can discuss possible next steps for these needs, but not every product or existing system can be repaired, integrated or retained. An existing smart home upgrade starts with understanding the current installation and what you want to change.

How Smart-Home Controls and Automations Work

A scene is a saved set of actions for compatible devices, such as adjusting lights and curtains together. A keypad, app or sensor can trigger a scene or another automation; only devices that are compatible and correctly integrated will respond. When comparing systems, check which controls are available and whether the household can use them without relying on a single person’s phone.

Internet access and local operation are not the same. Automations processed by a local controller may continue during an internet outage if the controller, network and devices remain powered; remote access and cloud-dependent services may not. Ask which functions run locally, which depend on the cloud, and what will still work if the internet connection is lost.
