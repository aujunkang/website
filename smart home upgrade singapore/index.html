06 / Frequently Asked Questions

Questions before you upgrade.

Can HAP work on a smart home installed by another company?

Yes. HAP has experience taking over existing installations from other providers, including The Marq. The available upgrade options depend on the equipment, system access and compatibility.

Can my older Control4 system be upgraded?

HAP can assess an existing Control4 system and recommend suitable controller, software, hardware or programming improvements. Compatibility and the current system configuration determine the upgrade options.

Can existing smart home wiring be reused?

Existing wiring can sometimes be reused when its type, condition and installation route meet the requirements of the proposed equipment. Some projects require additional cabling or electrical work.

Can I retrofit a smart home in Singapore without major renovation?

Many smart home improvements can be planned around existing infrastructure. Programming changes and selected equipment replacements may involve less disruption, while concealed cabling or extensive lighting changes can require additional work.

Can CCTV be added to an existing home without camera cables?

HAP can plan additional CCTV locations and assess potential cable routes, equipment positions and power requirements. The installation approach depends on the property layout and available access.

Do I need to replace my entire smart home system?

Not always. HAP evaluates the existing installation to identify which components may be retained and which should be updated or replaced to achieve the intended improvements.

How much does a smart home upgrade cost in Singapore?

Costs depend on the existing equipment, programming requirements, compatibility, new cabling and installation scope. Explore HAP's smart home cost guide for general budget considerations.
