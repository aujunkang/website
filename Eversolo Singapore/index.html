Quick Comparison

Eversolo Model Comparison for Singapore Buyers

Use this as a simple guide before speaking with HAP. The right Eversolo model depends on whether you need an amplifier, DAC, streamer or transport.

Eversolo Play vs DMP-A6 Gen 2

Choose Eversolo Play if you want an all-in-one streaming amplifier that can drive passive speakers. Choose DMP-A6 Gen 2 if you already have an amplifier or active speakers and want a dedicated streamer and DAC.

DMP-A6 Gen 2 vs DMP-A8 Gen 2

DMP-A6 Gen 2 is the more accessible route into serious Hi-Fi streaming. DMP-A8 Gen 2 steps up to a larger 8.6-inch interface, flagship AKM conversion, a fully balanced analogue preamp, more system connectivity and up to 8TB SSD expansion.

DMP-A8 Gen 2 vs DMP-A8 Master Gen 2

Both share the flagship AKM DAC platform and core streaming features. Choose DMP-A8 Gen 2 if streaming, DAC and preamp duties are your priority. Choose DMP-A8 Master Gen 2 if you also want integrated CD playback and ripping, upgraded OCXO clocking and support for an external 10MHz master clock.

DMP-A8 Gen 2 vs DMP-A10

DMP-A8 Gen 2 is a strong choice for premium two-channel systems that need a modern streamer, DAC and preamp in one. DMP-A10 remains the higher-tier option for flagship systems where source quality, system flexibility and a more ambitious component platform are the priority.

Eversolo T8 vs DMP-A8 Gen 2

Choose Eversolo T8 if you already own a high-quality external DAC and mainly need a dedicated streaming transport. Choose DMP-A8 Gen 2 if you want streaming, DAC conversion and analogue preamplifier functions together in one component.
