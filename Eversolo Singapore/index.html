Quick Comparison

Eversolo Model Comparison for Singapore Buyers

The right Eversolo model depends on the role it needs to perform in your system: all-in-one amplifier, streamer and DAC, streamer/DAC/preamplifier, or dedicated digital transport for an external DAC.

Eversolo Play vs DMP-A6 Gen 2

Choose Eversolo Play if you want an all-in-one streaming amplifier that can drive passive speakers. Choose DMP-A6 Gen 2 if you already have an amplifier or active speakers and want a dedicated streamer and DAC.

DMP-A6 Gen 2 vs DMP-A6 Master Edition Gen 2

Both models cover the same core streamer, DAC and preamplifier role. DMP-A6 Master Edition Gen 2 builds on the standard DMP-A6 Gen 2 platform with dual ultra-low phase-noise femtosecond audio clocks. Choose the standard model for the core Gen 2 functions, or the Master Edition when upgraded clocking is a priority.

DMP-A8 vs DMP-A8 Gen 2

DMP-A8 is the earlier generation in Eversolo's A8 line. DMP-A8 Gen 2 updates the platform with an 8.6-inch touchscreen, redesigned interface, Wi-Fi 6 and newer processing architecture, while retaining the AK4191EQ + AK4499EX family of DAC architecture.

DMP-A6 Gen 2 vs DMP-A8 Gen 2

DMP-A6 Gen 2 is a compact streamer, DAC and preamplifier for existing stereo systems. DMP-A8 Gen 2 moves to a larger 8.6-inch touchscreen, AK4191EQ + AK4499EXEQ DAC architecture, balanced analogue preamplifier design, expanded connectivity and support for an internal NVMe SSD up to 8TB.

DMP-A8 Gen 2 vs DMP-A8 Master Gen 2

Both are built around streaming, DAC and analogue preamplifier functions. Choose DMP-A8 Gen 2 if those core functions are your priority. Choose DMP-A8 Master Gen 2 if you also want integrated CD playback together with the Master Edition clocking platform.

DMP-A8 Gen 2 vs DMP-A10

DMP-A8 Gen 2 is designed to combine streaming, DAC conversion and analogue preamplifier functions in one component. DMP-A10 sits higher in the Eversolo range and is aimed at more ambitious Hi-Fi systems where the source component and overall system architecture are higher priorities.

Eversolo T8 vs DMP-A8 Gen 2

Choose Eversolo T8 if you already own a high-quality external DAC and mainly need a dedicated streaming transport. Choose DMP-A8 Gen 2 if you want streaming, DAC conversion and analogue preamplifier functions together in one component.

Which Eversolo model should I choose?

Choose Eversolo Play if you want streaming and speaker amplification in one unit. Choose a DMP streamer such as DMP-A6 Gen 2 or DMP-A8 Gen 2 if you already use separate amplification or compatible active speakers. Consider DMP-A10 for a higher-tier source component, and choose T8 if you already own an external DAC and mainly need a dedicated streaming transport.

What is the difference between Eversolo Play and DMP-A6 Gen 2?

Eversolo Play is an all-in-one streaming amplifier that can drive compatible passive speakers. Eversolo DMP-A6 Gen 2 is a music streamer, DAC and preamplifier for systems that use separate amplification or compatible active speakers.

What is the difference between Eversolo DMP-A6 Gen 2 and DMP-A8 Gen 2?

Eversolo DMP-A6 Gen 2 combines network streaming, DAC conversion and preamplifier functions in a compact component. Eversolo DMP-A8 Gen 2 is a larger platform with an 8.6-inch touchscreen, AK4191EQ + AK4499EXEQ DAC architecture, balanced analogue preamplifier design and broader system connectivity.

Should I choose Eversolo DMP-A8 Gen 2 or DMP-A10?

Eversolo DMP-A8 Gen 2 combines streaming, DAC conversion and analogue preamplifier functions in one component. Eversolo DMP-A10 sits higher in the Eversolo range and is intended for more ambitious Hi-Fi systems where the source component and system architecture are greater priorities.

Do I need a separate amplifier with Eversolo?

It depends on the model. Eversolo Play includes power amplification and can drive compatible passive speakers directly. Eversolo DMP streamers are source, DAC and preamplifier components rather than speaker power amplifiers, so passive speakers normally require separate amplification. Eversolo T8 is a streaming transport and is intended for systems that already use an external DAC and amplification.

What is the difference between Eversolo DMP-A6 Gen 2 and DMP-A6 Master Edition Gen 2?

DMP-A6 Master Edition Gen 2 builds on the DMP-A6 Gen 2 platform with dual ultra-low phase-noise femtosecond audio clocks. The standard DMP-A6 Gen 2 remains the simpler choice when you want the core streamer, DAC and preamplifier functions without specifically stepping up to the Master Edition.

What is the difference between Eversolo DMP-A8 and DMP-A8 Gen 2?

DMP-A8 is the earlier-generation A8 model. DMP-A8 Gen 2 introduces an 8.6-inch touchscreen, redesigned interface, Wi-Fi 6 and newer processing architecture, while continuing the A8 concept of combining streaming, DAC and analogue preamplifier functions.

What is Eversolo T8 used for?

Eversolo T8 is a dedicated streaming transport. It is best for Hi-Fi systems that already have a high-quality external DAC and only need a clean digital streaming source.

Can Eversolo work with Bowers & Wilkins speakers?

Yes. Eversolo can be used as the digital source in a Bowers & Wilkins system. Passive Bowers & Wilkins speakers still need suitable amplification when used with Eversolo DMP streamers or T-series transports, while Eversolo Play includes its own power amplification for a simpler compatible passive-speaker setup.
