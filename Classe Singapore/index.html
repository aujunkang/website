Classé Delta Range

Classé Delta PRE Mk II and Delta STEREO

The Delta PRE Mk II manages sources, volume and signal routing. The Delta STEREO supplies the power that drives the loudspeakers. Together, they form the control-and-amplification foundation of a serious two-channel system.

This page gives the buying overview. The dedicated product pages contain deeper model information and should be used when you need full specifications.

Preamplifier and Control

Classé Delta PRE Mk II

The Delta PRE Mk II is the system control centre for source selection, volume and signal routing. It includes HEOS streaming, network and USB playback, digital inputs, an onboard DAC, MM/MC phono support and mono or stereo subwoofer management.

Two-Channel Power

Classé Delta STEREO

The Delta STEREO is rated at 250 watts per channel into 8 ohms and 500 watts per channel into 4 ohms. Its first 12.5 watts per channel operate in Class A.
